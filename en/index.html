About

I love turning an idea into a visual that tells it.

I’m a third-year Visual Communication Design student at Istanbul Medeniyet University. I work across graphic design and videography — print pieces like magazines, brochures, book covers and brand identities, alongside video editing and motion content. Lately I’ve been bringing the same design eye to web apps I build myself.

I care about function as much as aesthetics. I’m detail-oriented, open to growth and I simply love making things.
